## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Greenville

We start with an assessment that separates dry smoke residue (typically from fast, hot fires) from wet, greasy smoke film (common with smoldering fires in older insulation or wood framing), because the cleaning agents and techniques differ. Hard surfaces get HEPA vacuuming and dry sponge treatment before any wet cleaning touches them, since wiping dry soot with a damp cloth just grinds it deeper into plaster and grout. For odor, we use thermal fogging or ozone treatment in unoccupied structures, paired with HVAC duct cleaning, since Greenville's older forced-air systems and radiator setups both tend to recirculate smoke particulate for weeks if the ductwork and registers aren't addressed directly. Contents that can be salvaged, upholstery, textiles, electronics, go through separate cleaning and deodorization rather than treatment in place, which matters more here than in newer construction because original woodwork and built-ins are often worth restoring rather than replacing.
