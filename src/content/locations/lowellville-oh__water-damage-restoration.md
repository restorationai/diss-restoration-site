## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Lowellville

The first step on any call is stopping the source and assessing how far water has traveled, which in Lowellville's older homes often means checking behind plaster and lath walls, not just drywall. Plaster absorbs moisture more slowly than drywall but holds onto it longer, so we adjust drying timelines accordingly rather than pulling equipment too early because surface readings look dry. Extraction comes next, using truck-mounted or portable pumps depending on how much standing water has pooled in a basement or crawlspace, followed by moisture mapping to find out whether water has migrated into subfloor framing, which is common in houses with the kind of fieldstone or parged-block foundations typical of this area. Structural drying is set up and monitored daily, with psychrometric readings logged so the file matches IICRC S500 drying standards insurance adjusters expect to see.
