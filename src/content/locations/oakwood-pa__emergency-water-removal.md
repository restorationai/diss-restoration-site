## Our Emergency Water Removal & Cleanup Process in Oakwood

## Oakwood Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most homeowner policies cover sudden water damage like a burst pipe or failed appliance supply line, but they draw a hard line around gradual leaks or long-term seepage, which is where documentation becomes critical. We photograph and log moisture readings from the first visit so there's a clear timeline if your adjuster asks whether the loss was sudden or ongoing. If your property is part of an association with shared plumbing stacks or common drainage systems, we coordinate directly with property management so repairs on shared infrastructure don't stall the drying timeline on your unit.
