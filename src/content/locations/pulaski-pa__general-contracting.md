## Why Pulaski Homes Need a Different Remodeling Approach

Pulaski is a small Lawrence County borough built mostly around farmhouses, mid-century ranch homes, and converted outbuildings on larger rural lots. That housing stock comes with quirks a suburban contractor doesn't always plan for: balloon-framed walls from an earlier construction era, plumbing runs that were patched generation after generation, and well-and-septic systems instead of municipal hookups. A bathroom remodel here isn't just tile and fixtures, it's often rerouting supply lines that were never sized for a modern shower, or confirming the septic tank location before anyone pours a new slab. Kitchen remodels frequently run into the same issue: original wiring that predates the appliance load a remodeled kitchen is expected to carry.

## Our Renovations, Remodels and General Contracting Process in Pulaski

We start with a walkthrough that looks at structure first and finishes second. For post-damage rebuilds, that means confirming what water, fire, or mold already touched before we frame anything new, since covering a problem with drywall only delays it. From there we scope the project, pull the appropriate permits through Lawrence County, and sequence the trades so plumbing and electrical work happen before cabinetry or flooring go in. Our crews carry IICRC training from the restoration side of the business, which matters on a rebuild: a wall that was wet six weeks ago gets moisture-tested before we close it back up, not assumed dry because it looks fine.
