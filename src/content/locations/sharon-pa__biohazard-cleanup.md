## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Sharon

Every job starts with containment. We isolate the affected area to prevent cross-contamination into living spaces, then our OSHA-trained technicians remove and dispose of affected materials following state and federal regulations for biohazardous waste. Surfaces that can be saved are cleaned and disinfected using hospital-grade antimicrobial products, and we test for residual contamination before closing out a space. Sharps and needles, when present, are handled with dedicated puncture-resistant containers and logged for proper disposal. Throughout, we document everything with photos and written reports, both for your insurance file and for your own peace of mind that the work was done correctly.
