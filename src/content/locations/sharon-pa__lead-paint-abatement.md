## Why Sharon's Housing Stock Carries Lead Paint Risk

Sharon grew up fast during the steel era, and a large share of the city's homes, duplexes, and rowhouses were built or renovated in the decades before 1978, when lead-based residential paint was still standard. Layers of old paint on window casings, baseboards, and exterior clapboard in these Shenango Valley properties often sit on top of original lead coats rather than replacing them. Add in the freeze-thaw cycles common to this part of Mercer County, which cause exterior paint to chalk, crack, and shed fine dust off sills and porch rails, and you get a steady source of lead exposure that homeowners don't always notice until a renovation, a window replacement, or a routine lead test during a refinance or rental inspection flags it.

## Our Lead Paint Abatement Process in Sharon

We start with testing, not assumptions. A technician checks suspect surfaces, typically window components, doors, trim, and any chipping or peeling exterior paint, since those are the areas most likely to generate lead dust during normal wear. If lead is confirmed, the work area gets sealed with containment plastic and negative air pressure where needed, following EPA RRP work practices for disturbing lead paint. We use wet methods and HEPA-filtered equipment to keep dust from migrating into living spaces or outside onto soil near the foundation, which matters in older Sharon neighborhoods where kids and pets use the yard. Once the paint is removed or encapsulated, the space gets a HEPA vacuum pass and wet wipe-down, and we document the work for your records, which matters if the property is a rental subject to lead-safe housing rules.

## Local note

One thing that catches Sharon property owners off guard: a lot of the city's older double-hung windows were repainted multiple times over the decades without ever being fully stripped, so what looks like a single top coat of white or green paint can actually be eight or ten layers deep, with the lead sitting two or three coats down. Simple sanding during a DIY window repair can release far more lead dust than a homeowner expects, which is why testing before any trim or window work on a pre-1978 Sharon property is worth the extra step.
