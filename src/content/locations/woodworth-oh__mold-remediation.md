## Why Woodworth Properties See Mold Issues

Much of the housing stock in this part of Ohio was built with block or poured-concrete foundations that were never paired with modern vapor barriers. Combine that with a region that swings from humid, sticky summers to damp, gray winters, and basements and crawlspaces end up holding moisture longer than the rest of the house. Add an undetected roof leak, a slow plumbing drip behind a tub surround, or a dryer vent that's been disconnected for years, and you've got the conditions mold needs. We see it most often in lower levels, around window wells, and in bathrooms with poor ventilation, which is why our inspection always starts at the building envelope and works inward rather than assuming the visible spot is the whole problem.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Woodworth

We start with a moisture map, using meters to find where water is actually traveling, since mold on a ceiling can be fed by a leak several feet away. Once we've confirmed the scope, we isolate the work area with physical containment and negative air pressure so spores don't migrate into the rest of the home while material is removed. Affected drywall, insulation, or subflooring gets cut back to sound material, surfaces are treated per IICRC S520 guidance, and air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run until particle counts come down. For older Ohio homes with plaster or full masonry basements, we adjust dry times because those materials release moisture slower than drywall, even after the surface looks dry. Our AMRT-trained technicians document every step with photos for your records and for your insurance carrier if the claim involves a covered water loss that led to the mold.

## Local note

One thing we watch for in this part of Ohio: homes with finished basements built in the 1990s and 2000s often have carpet laid directly over concrete with no vapor barrier underneath. It looks fine for years, then a humid summer or a sump pump that can't quite keep up lets moisture wick up through the slab, and mold shows up on the underside of the carpet pad before anyone smells it in the room above. If your basement has original carpet from that era and you've noticed a musty smell without visible staining, that's usually where we check first.
