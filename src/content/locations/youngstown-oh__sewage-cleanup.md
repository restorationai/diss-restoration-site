## Why Youngstown Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Beyond aging clay pipe, a lot of Youngstown's older homes were built with basement floor drains tied directly into the main sewer line with no backwater valve. When the municipal line or a shared lateral backs up, that drain becomes the point of entry instead of the point of exit. Older multi-family conversions and duplexes, common in neighborhoods built up during the city's industrial peak, sometimes share a single lateral between units, which means a clog or root intrusion on a neighbor's side of the line can send sewage into a property that otherwise has no plumbing problem of its own.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process

We start by identifying the contamination category under IICRC S500 guidelines before touching anything, since raw sewage is classified as the most hazardous water category and dictates how materials are handled. Porous materials that have been in contact with sewage, carpet pad, drywall below the flood line, and most insulation, typically cannot be sanitized back to a safe condition and get removed rather than dried in place. We extract standing wastewater, remove and bag contaminated materials for proper disposal, and then apply EPA-registered antimicrobial treatments to the subfloor, framing, and any remaining structural surfaces. Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run throughout the sanitization phase to manage airborne particulates, and we verify drying with moisture meters before any rebuild begins, consistent with OSHA-trained handling protocols for contaminated water work.
