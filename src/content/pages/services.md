DISS Restoration handles the full arc of property damage recovery: the emergency mitigation that stops a loss from getting worse, and the reconstruction that brings a property back to pre-loss condition. Crews work across Farrell and the surrounding Shenango Valley for homeowners, landlords, and property managers, with the same crew structure whether the job is a single-family basement or a multi-unit apartment building. Below is a quick map of what falls under each service area; each has its own page with more detail.

## Emergency restoration services

Water damage restoration covers everything from a failed sump pump to a supply line burst, with extraction, structural drying per the IICRC S500 standard, and monitoring until moisture readings confirm the structure is dry. Fire and smoke damage restoration addresses soot removal, smoke odor that has worked into drywall and fabric, and the soot residue left on surfaces after a fire is out. Biohazard and trauma cleanup is handled with the same discretion and protective protocol as any other hazardous-material job. Storm damage response covers wind, hail, and flooding impacts, and emergency board-up or tarping secures a damaged roof or window opening against weather and intrusion while repairs are scheduled. Because DISS Restoration answers 24/7, these services start with a phone call, not a form.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Once a structure is dry and stable, reconstruction picks up where mitigation leaves off: framing, drywall, flooring, trim, and paint, rebuilt to match the rest of the property rather than patched as an afterthought. General contracting work extends beyond damage repair into broader renovation scope when a project calls for it. Keeping reconstruction under the same roof as mitigation means one point of contact through the whole timeline, one set of documentation for the insurance file, and no gap where a homeowner has to vet a second contractor mid-project.

## Specialty services

Mold inspection and remediation addresses growth that often follows water intrusion once moisture sits in a wall cavity or crawlspace long enough to colonize, handled by crews trained under IICRC AMRT protocols. Odor removal targets smoke, pet, or moisture odors that linger after the visible damage is gone. Additional specialty work includes contents cleaning and structural drying support for jobs that don't fit neatly into a single category. Lead-safe work practices apply on any project involving older building materials, consistent with EPA Lead-Safe certification.

Most property damage doesn't respect the line between mitigation and reconstruction, which is exactly why DISS Restoration keeps both under one roof. A single call starts the assessment, and the same company that extracts the water or boards the window is the one framing the new drywall weeks later.
