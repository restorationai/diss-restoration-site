## Restoration emergencies common in Lowellville

Lowellville sits along the Mahoning River in Mahoning County, and that proximity shapes a lot of the water damage calls we see from this part of the valley. Add in Ohio's freeze-thaw winters, where pipes in unheated crawlspaces or exterior walls are vulnerable to bursting once temperatures swing, and you get a steady mix of water intrusion calls from both weather and plumbing failures. Water damage restoration is our most frequent call in Lowellville, and it's rarely just about pulling standing water out of a basement. Mold remediation becomes necessary when moisture sits unaddressed, which happens often in basements and crawlspaces that take on water quietly over weeks before anyone notices the smell or the staining. Much of Lowellville's housing dates to the first half of the 20th century, when the village grew around its steel and rail industry, and that means a mix of block and stone foundations, balloon-frame construction, and basements rather than slab-on-grade.
