## Restoration emergencies common in McKinley Heights

McKinley Heights sits in a part of Ohio that swings hard between seasons, and that swing is what drives most of the calls we get. Winter freeze-thaw cycles crack supply lines that then fail quietly inside a wall until a warm spell lets the leak show itself. Spring brings heavy rain on ground that's often still partially frozen underneath, which pushes water toward foundations and basements faster than it can drain. Summer humidity turns any missed moisture, behind drywall, under carpet pad, inside a crawlspace, into a mold problem within days rather than weeks. Add in older housing stock with basements that were never meant to handle sustained water exposure, and you get a steady mix of pipe failures, sump pump overwhelm, and seasonal mold calls year-round.
