## Restoration emergencies common in New Castle Northwest

New Castle sits where the Shenango River and Mahoning River meet to form the Beaver River, and the northwest side of the city feels that geography directly. Properties closer to the river corridor see higher water tables and slower-draining soil, which means basements and crawlspaces take on water not just from storms but from seasonal snowmelt and spring runoff. Add in a housing stock that's largely 70 to 100 years old, and you get plumbing that was never built for today's water pressure, roofs past their expected service life, and foundations that have settled enough to let groundwater find a way in. Winters bring freeze-thaw cycles that crack pipes and foundations alike, while summer thunderstorms can overwhelm older drainage and stormwater infrastructure faster than it was designed to handle.
