## Restoration emergencies common in North Lima

North Lima sits in Mahoning County's mix of open farmland and low-density residential development, which means a lot of homes rely on well water, septic fields, or sump pumps rather than municipal systems built to handle heavy runoff. When a hard freeze hits and then breaks fast, the ground can't absorb the melt quickly enough, and basements and crawlspaces take on water through foundation cracks and old tile drains. Thunderstorms and the occasional remnant of a larger system can also drop enough rain in a short window to overwhelm older drainage, especially on properties with mature trees and established root systems near foundations. Add in the number of homes still running original plumbing from decades back, and pipe failures (frozen, corroded, or just old) are a steady driver of water calls alongside storm-related damage.

## Services we provide in North Lima

Water damage in North Lima often starts below grade, in a basement or crawlspace, where it can sit against foundation walls and framing longer than a homeowner realizes before it's noticed upstairs.
